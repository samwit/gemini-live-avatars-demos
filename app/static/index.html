✨ Gemini Live Avatar demos

Real-time talking avatars,
driven by Gemini 3.8 Live.

Each demo streams your voice (and optionally your camera) to Gemini and gets back a lip-synced video of an avatar that talks, listens, uses tools and can be interrupted. Pick one below.

Prefer the terminal? Try scripts/01_hello_avatar.py (smallest possible example), scripts/02_raw_websocket.py (the wire protocol, no SDK), and scripts/03_script_to_video.py (turn a script into a talking-head MP4).
